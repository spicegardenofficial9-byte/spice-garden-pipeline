DISH: Paneer Butter Masala (Punjabi / North Indian)
TITLE: Authentic Paneer Butter Masala at Home! 🧈✨ #shorts

INGREDIENTS:
  - Paneer
  - Butter
  - Onions
  - Tomatoes
  - Cashews
  - Ginger
  - Garlic
  - Cardamom
  - Cloves
  - Kashmiri red chili powder
  - Garam masala
  - Salt
  - Kasuri methi
  - Fresh cream
  - Fresh cilantro

FULL NARRATION:
Stop buying restaurant paneer butter masala when you can make it better at home! First, sauté onions, tomatoes, cashews, ginger, garlic, and whole spices in butter until soft. Blend this into a silky smooth puree. Pour it back into melted butter, then add Kashmiri red chili powder, garam masala, and salt. Toss in fresh paneer cubes and simmer gently. Finish with crushed kasuri methi and fresh cream. Subscribe to Spice Garden for more authentic recipes!
