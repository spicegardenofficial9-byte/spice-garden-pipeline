SPICE GARDEN - SUBSCRIBE ANIMATION (make this ONCE, reused on every video)
============================================================================

Generate a single ~4-second vertical (9:16) subscribe animation, save it as:

    assets/branding/subscribe.mp4

The pipeline appends it (crossfaded, with its own audio) to the END of every
video automatically - so it only needs to be made once. Keep it on-brand:
warm, hand-painted Studio-Ghibli look, cozy Indian-kitchen palette
(saffron / warm gold / deep green), matching the videos.

MUST include (all of these):
- A gold notification BELL icon that animates: it drops/pops in, then RINGS
  (wobbles/shakes side to side a couple of times), with small motion lines or
  a soft sparkle on the ring.
- A "SUBSCRIBE" button/pill (classic red) that pops in, and a short line of
  text above or below it, e.g. "Subscribe for more" / "More recipes daily".
- SOUND: a clear BELL "ding"/chime sound effect synced to the moment the bell
  rings (the audio is kept and plays in the final video). A soft whoosh/pop on
  the button is a nice extra. This bell sound is required.
- Duration ~4 seconds, 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 (or upscaled to it).
- Keep the bottom-right corner clear (that corner may carry a generator
  watermark on the main clips; the subscribe screen should not clutter it).

PROMPT you can paste into your animation / video tool:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
A 4-second vertical 9:16 subscribe end-screen in a warm hand-painted Studio
Ghibli style, cozy Indian kitchen colours (saffron, warm gold, deep green). A
gold notification bell icon pops into the centre and rings - wobbling side to
side with tiny motion lines and a soft sparkle - while a classic red
"SUBSCRIBE" button pops in beneath it with the words "Subscribe for more".
Gentle, satisfying motion; clean and uncluttered; keep the bottom-right corner
empty. Audio: a bright, clear bell "ding" chime synced to the bell ringing,
plus a soft pop/whoosh as the button appears.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Notes:
- Any short clip works as long as it ends cleanly; it will be capped at
  config.SUBSCRIBE_CTA_DURATION_SEC (~4s) and scaled to 1080x1920.
- To change it later, just replace assets/branding/subscribe.mp4 - every
  future build picks up the new one. No code change needed.
